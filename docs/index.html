Your districts and an AI prompt

1 Find your state House and Senate races

Most of the ballot is the same for everyone in Lakewood. Your state House and Senate races depend on where you live.

Your address is sent only to the U.S. Census Bureau's address lookup to find your districts. We don't keep it.

Or look up your exact ballot at GoVoteColorado.gov, or read the Jefferson County sample ballot.

2 Prefer to ask an AI? Copy this into ChatGPT, Claude, or any assistant:

Help me understand my ballot for the November 3, 2026 election in Lakewood, Colorado. I've attached llms-full.txt, the full Lakewood Votes guide. Use only that file and the sources it links. Ask me what matters to me, then walk me through each race and measure, with links to the sources.

Your districts from step 1 are included, so the assistant won't need to ask. Your address is not.

Testing locally: attach llms-full.txt (in your docs folder) to the chat along with this prompt.

AI assistants can get things wrong. Every claim on this site links to its source, so check the links.
