Past elections

Who ran, who won, and how the measures did in recent elections on Lakewood ballots. Every number links to the official count it came from.

April 2026

April 7, 2026 Lakewood special election (zoning referendum)

Lakewood voters decided four referendum questions on whether to repeal the four ordinances in the City Council's 2025 zoning code update. Voters repealed all four, each by about 62% to 38%, so the prior zoning code remains in effect.

Results: 4 measures2025

November 4, 2025 Lakewood city election (coordinated election)

Lakewood voters chose City Council members in all five wards; four incumbents were reelected and Liz Black won the open Ward 2 seat. Voters also chose three Jeffco Public Schools board members and decided two statewide measures, Propositions LL and MM, which both passed. There were no City of Lakewood ballot questions.

Results: 8 races, 2 measures2024

November 5, 2024 general election

Lakewood voters chose a president, their member of Congress (CD-7), a CU regent, state representatives and two Jefferson County commissioners, and voted on 14 statewide measures. Locally, voters approved Lakewood Ballot Issue 2A, which lets the city keep revenue above its TABOR limit permanently, along with Jefferson County 1A and RTD 7A, which made similar changes for the county and RTD.

Results: 11 races, 17 measures2023

November 7, 2023 Lakewood city election

Lakewood voters elected Wendi Strom mayor and filled one City Council seat in each of the five wards, with no incumbents on the ballot in any council race, and voted to retain two municipal judges. Jeffco Public Schools voters filled two school board seats (Districts 3 and 4). Statewide, Proposition HH (property tax changes) failed and Proposition II (keeping nicotine tax revenue for preschool) passed.

Results: 8 races, 2 measures
