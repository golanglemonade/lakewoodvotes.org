November 5, 2024 general election

Lakewood voters chose a president, their member of Congress (CD-7), a CU regent, state representatives and two Jefferson County commissioners, and voted on 14 statewide measures. Locally, voters approved Lakewood Ballot Issue 2A, which lets the city keep revenue above its TABOR limit permanently, along with Jefferson County 1A and RTD 7A, which made similar changes for the county and RTD.

Turnout: Jefferson County: 367,408 ballots from 437,432 active voters (about 84%). Lakewood (Ballot Issue 2A contest): 89,396 ballots from 111,507 registered voters (80.2%). Statewide: 3,240,549 ballots (79.5% of active voters). Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Secretary of State

Vote counts are copied from the cited official reports. Where Lakewood-only numbers weren't published, the count is for all of Jefferson County, and the race notes say so.

Ballot Issue 2A Keep revenue above TABOR limits permanently
What it did

Let the city keep and spend all revenue above its TABOR limit starting in 2026, with no new or higher tax. The extra money is split equally among parks, recreation and open space; public safety; and streets, sidewalks, paths and infrastructure, and kept in a separate account. A 2017 voter approval to keep this revenue was set to expire in 2025. Jefferson County count: 89,396 ballots cast of 111,507 registered Lakewood voters.

Ballot Issue 1A County keeps revenue above TABOR limits
What it did

Let Jefferson County keep and spend all revenue from its existing sources starting in fiscal year 2024, without raising any tax or mill levy. The money goes to roads, bridges and other infrastructure and to public safety, including wildfire and flood work and mental health and addiction programs, with yearly review by an independent auditor and a citizen committee.

Good to know

  • Jefferson County counted 367,408 ballots from 437,432 active voters (about 84%): 342,033 by mail and 25,363 in person. The county canvass certified the results on Nov. 27, 2024. Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)
  • In the Lakewood Ballot Issue 2A contest, 89,396 ballots were counted from 111,507 registered Lakewood voters (80.2%). Source: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder
  • Statewide, 3,240,549 of 4,074,680 active voters cast ballots (79.5%); 92.2% voted by mail. The Secretary of State certified the statewide results on Dec. 6, 2024. Source: Colorado Secretary of State
  • No state Senate race covering Lakewood (Senate Districts 20 and 22) was on the 2024 ballot; the only Jefferson County senate contests were Districts 16, 19 and 26. Source: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder
  • Lakewood voters had no State Board of Education race in 2024. The only board race on Jefferson County ballots was for Congressional District 2, which does not include Lakewood. Source: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder
  • The certified county report lists no Jeffco Public Schools ballot measure in November 2024. Source: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder
  • Colorado voters decided 14 statewide measures in 2024: seven constitutional amendments (G, H, I, J, K, 79, 80) and seven statutory propositions (JJ, KK, 127, 128, 129, 130, 131). Amendments needed 55% to pass. Source: Ballotpedia
  • Every judge on the Jefferson County ballot received a majority 'yes' vote in Jefferson County. Source: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder
