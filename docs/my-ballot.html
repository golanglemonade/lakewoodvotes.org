Your sample ballot

Everything on a Lakewood ballot, in the order it appears, with bubbles you can fill in. Mark your choices, check them, then print this page and take it with you when you fill out your real ballot.

Show only your districts

Lakewood is split among several state House and Senate districts. Enter your address and this page will show only yours.

Your address is sent only to the U.S. Census Bureau's address lookup to find your districts. We don't keep it.
