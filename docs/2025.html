November 4, 2025 Lakewood city election (coordinated election)

Lakewood voters chose City Council members in all five wards; four incumbents were reelected and Liz Black won the open Ward 2 seat. Voters also chose three Jeffco Public Schools board members and decided two statewide measures, Propositions LL and MM, which both passed. There were no City of Lakewood ballot questions.

Turnout: In Lakewood's five wards, 45,817 ballots were cast out of 112,168 registered voters (about 40.85%, summed from county ward totals). Jefferson County overall: 199,921 of 440,729 (45.36%). Statewide: 1,716,655 of 4,135,705 active voters (41.51%). Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Secretary of State

Vote counts are copied from the cited official reports. Where Lakewood-only numbers weren't published, the count is for all of Jefferson County, and the race notes say so.

Proposition MM Further limit income tax deductions for high earners to fund school meals and food aid
What it did

Asked whether to raise about $95 million a year by limiting state income tax deductions to $1,000 for single filers or $2,000 for joint filers with federal taxable income of $300,000 or more. The money goes to the school meals program, wages for school food workers, and SNAP food assistance. Totals shown are statewide certified results.

Good to know

  • There were no City of Lakewood ballot questions, no Jefferson County measures, and no Jeffco Public Schools measures on the November 2025 ballot. The special-district measures on the county ballot (Lookout Mountain Water, Mountain Shadows Metro, South Metro Fire Rescue, Coal Creek Canyon Fire) were for other areas. Source: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder
  • Lakewood holds regular city elections in November of odd-numbered years. The mayor and council members serve four-year terms. The mayor was not on the 2025 ballot; Mayor Wendi Strom's term runs November 2023 to November 2027. Sources: City of Lakewood (1, 2)
  • Adding the five ward totals in the county's statement of votes cast: 45,817 ballots were cast in Lakewood's wards out of 112,168 registered voters (about 40.85%). Source: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder
  • Jefferson County: 199,921 ballots cast out of 440,729 registered voters (45.36%). The Clerk reports 196,849 voted by mail and 2,979 in person. The county canvass certified results on November 25, 2025. Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)
  • Statewide, 1,716,655 ballots were cast out of 4,135,705 active voters (41.51%). The Secretary of State certified results on November 26, 2025. Source: Colorado Secretary of State
  • New and reelected council members were sworn in on November 24, 2025: Jeslin Shahrezaei, Liz Black, Ken Cruz, Bill Furman and Paula Nystrom. Source: Denver Gazette
  • The three newly elected Jeffco school board members (Echevarria, Gibbins, Moeinian) were scheduled to be sworn in on December 3, 2025, to four-year terms. Source: Jeffco Public Schools
  • On October 13, 2025, the City Council gave final approval to a nearly 400-page zoning code update, made up of four ordinances (2025-27, 2025-28, 2025-29 and 2025-30). The new rules replaced single-family-only zones with residential zones that allow more than one home per lot. Residents then petitioned to refer all four ordinances to voters, which led to the April 7, 2026 special election. Sources: Colorado Politics / Denver Gazette; Jeffco Transcript / Colorado Community Media; Denverite
