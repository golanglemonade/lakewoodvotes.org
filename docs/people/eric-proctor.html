Eric Proctor

On the November 3, 2026 ballot for Jefferson County Treasurer. Ballot position 1.

About

Eric Proctor is the Democratic nominee for Jefferson County Treasurer. He describes himself as a community banker and a former public school teacher, and he has held Democratic Party posts including Jefferson County Democratic Party secretary.

Sources: Arvadans For Progressive Action; The Critical Report; Jefferson County Democratic Party
