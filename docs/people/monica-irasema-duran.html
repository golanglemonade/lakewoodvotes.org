Monica Irasema Duran

On the November 3, 2026 ballot for Colorado State Senator, State Senate District 22. Ballot position 2.

About

Democratic state representative since 2019, for House District 24 (2019-2023) and House District 23 (2023-present), and House Majority Leader since 2023; she is term-limited in the House and running for the Senate District 22 seat held by term-limited Sen. Jessie Danielson. She previously served on the Wheat Ridge City Council and worked in the dental industry for more than 30 years.

Sources: Wikipedia (1, 2, 3); Duran for Colorado
