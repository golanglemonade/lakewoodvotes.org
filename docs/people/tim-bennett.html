Tim Bennett

On the November 3, 2026 ballot for U.S. Representative, Congressional District 7. Ballot position 1.

About

Tim Bennett is the Republican nominee and a retired U.S. Army officer who served nearly 30 years and retired in 2018. He was elected in 2025 to the Lewis-Palmer School District 38 Board of Education and lives in Monument.

Sources: Tim Bennett for Congress; CPR News (1, 2)
