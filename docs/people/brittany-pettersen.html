Brittany Pettersen

On the November 3, 2026 ballot for U.S. Representative, Congressional District 7. Incumbent. Ballot position 2.

About

Brittany Pettersen, a Democrat, is the incumbent U.S. representative for the 7th Congressional District, first elected in 2022, and is seeking a third term. She previously served in the Colorado House of Representatives (2013-2019) and the Colorado Senate (2019-2023). She grew up in and lives in Jefferson County.

Sources: Ballotpedia; CPR News (1, 2)
