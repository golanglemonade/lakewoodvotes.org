Rebekah Stewart

On the November 3, 2026 ballot for Colorado State Representative, State House District 30. Incumbent. Ballot position 1.

About

Democratic state representative for House District 30 since January 2025 (elected 2024), running for re-election. She is a board-certified music therapist and a former member of the Lakewood City Council.
