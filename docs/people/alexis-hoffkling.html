Democratic

Alexis Hoffkling

On the November 3, 2026 ballot for Colorado State Representative, State House District 23. Ballot position 1.

About

Democratic candidate for the open House District 23 seat, which term-limited Rep. Monica Duran is leaving. Her campaign says she is a family doctor at a community health center that is part of Denver Health, teaches medical students and residents, and lives in Wheat Ridge.

Sources: Wikipedia; Alexis for Colorado; Colorado Secretary of State
