Murl Hendrickson

On the November 3, 2026 ballot for Jefferson County Treasurer. Ballot position 2.

About

Murl Hendrickson is the Libertarian Party candidate for Jefferson County Treasurer. His campaign says he lives in Arvada, works as an associate financial representative, holds a B.A. in economics from the University of Colorado, and is completing Certified Financial Planner certification.

Sources: Libertarian Party; Murl Hendrickson campaign (1, 2); Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder
