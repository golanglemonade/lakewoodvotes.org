Nicole Hensel

On the November 3, 2026 ballot for Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder. Ballot position 2.

About

Nicole Hensel is the Democratic nominee for Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder. Her campaign describes her as a former teacher, organizer and nonprofit leader who has worked on election-protection and voting-policy efforts.

Sources: The Critical Report; Nicole Hensel campaign
