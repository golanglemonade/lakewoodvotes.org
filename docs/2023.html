November 7, 2023 Lakewood city election

Lakewood voters elected Wendi Strom mayor and filled one City Council seat in each of the five wards, with no incumbents on the ballot in any council race, and voted to retain two municipal judges. Jeffco Public Schools voters filled two school board seats (Districts 3 and 4). Statewide, Proposition HH (property tax changes) failed and Proposition II (keeping nicotine tax revenue for preschool) passed.

Turnout: Jefferson County's official canvass lists 207,052 ballots cast out of 437,532 registered voters countywide (47.32%). A Lakewood-only turnout figure was not found; the mayor's race recorded 44,843 votes, plus 2,523 undervotes and 4 overvotes. Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder; Colorado Community Media; City of Lakewood

Vote counts are copied from the cited official reports. Where Lakewood-only numbers weren't published, the count is for all of Jefferson County, and the race notes say so.

Proposition HH Property tax changes and state revenue limit
What it did

This would have lowered property taxes on homes and businesses, expanded property tax relief for seniors, and paid local governments and school districts back for lost revenue using part of the state's surplus, which would have reduced TABOR refunds. It failed statewide and in Jefferson County.

Proposition II Keep nicotine tax revenue for preschool
What it did

This let the state keep about $23.65 million in cigarette, tobacco and nicotine tax revenue that came in above the 2020 Proposition EE estimate, and spend it on the state's universal preschool program, rather than lowering tax rates and refunding the money. It did not raise taxes. It passed statewide and in Jefferson County.

Good to know

  • Proposition HH and II vote counts above are Jefferson County totals. Statewide, Proposition HH failed with 682,667 Yes (40.7%) and 995,259 No (59.3%), and Proposition II passed with 1,130,047 Yes (67.53%) and 543,405 No (32.47%). Sources: Colorado Secretary of State (1, 2)
  • The state's risk-limiting audit report lists slightly lower Jefferson County Prop HH totals (82,270 Yes, 122,416 No) than the Secretary of State's final results (82,272 Yes, 122,418 No). This archive uses the Secretary of State's final numbers. Sources: Colorado Secretary of State (1, 2)
  • No incumbent council member ran for re-election in 2023. One seat in each of the five wards was on the ballot, and three of the five races (Wards 3, 4 and 5) had only one candidate. Sources: Colorado Community Media; Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder
  • The election was run by mail. Ballots went out Oct. 16-20, 2023, and had to be received by 7 p.m. on Nov. 7. Source: City of Lakewood
  • Mayor Wendi Strom and council members Glenda Sinks (Ward 1), Roger Low (Ward 3), Dave Rein (Ward 4) and Jacob LaBure (Ward 5) were sworn in on Nov. 27, 2023. Isabel Cruz (Ward 2) was not present and was to be sworn in later. They replaced outgoing two-term Mayor Adam Paul and the outgoing council members. Source: Colorado Community Media
  • Strom's election as mayor left a Ward 5 council seat vacant. City Council called a special election for Jan. 30, 2024, to fill it. Source: Denver Gazette
  • Jefferson County's official canvass results for the 2023 coordinated election are dated Nov. 28, 2023. Source: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder
  • The Jefferson County Clerk reported that after a six-month turnout campaign, 2023 turnout rose from 43% to 47%, and that Jeffco was the only Colorado county to pass 200,000 returned ballots. Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)
  • The city's voter guide listed no Lakewood ballot issues or questions in 2023 other than the two judicial retention questions. Source: City of Lakewood
