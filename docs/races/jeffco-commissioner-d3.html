Commissioner District 3

Jefferson County Commissioner

Who votes
All Lakewood voters (all Jefferson County voters vote for this seat, even though the winner must live in District 3)
How to vote
Vote for one · 4-year term

What the job is

The three county commissioners run Jefferson County government. They set the county budget, pass county rules, and oversee county services like roads, parks, and human services.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder; Jefferson County (1, 2); Denver Gazette; The Critical Report

Who's running

2 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Stephanie VandenBerg

Republican Party

Stephanie VandenBerg is the Republican nominee for Jefferson County Commissioner, District 3. She is a certified public accountant who owns a small business in Jefferson County, and she previously served as treasurer of a Jeffco charter school. Her campaign says her family has lived in Jefferson County since 2014 and in Deer Creek Canyon since 2018.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Ballot position 2

Lisa A. Cutter

Democratic Party

Lisa Cutter, a Democrat, is a Colorado state senator representing Senate District 20 (since January 2023) and previously served in the Colorado House, District 25, from 2019 to 2023. Her campaign says she runs a communications firm and lives in Jefferson County.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Worth knowing

Open seat. Commissioner Lesley Dahlkemper (District 3) chose not to run again; she is also the Democratic nominee for lieutenant governor. Lisa Cutter is a state senator. Only District 3 is on the 2026 ballot; Districts 1 and 2 are not.
