Jefferson County offices

Jefferson County Assessor

Who votes
All Lakewood voters
How to vote
Vote for one · 4-year term

What the job is

The assessor sets the value of homes, businesses, and land for property tax purposes.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder; Jefferson County (1, 2); The Critical Report; The Ballot Brief

Who's running

3 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Vera King

Republican Party

Vera King is the Republican nominee for Jefferson County Assessor. The Critical Report describes her as a community advocate.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Ballot position 2

Andy Kerr

Democratic Party

Andy Kerr, a Democrat, is the Jefferson County Commissioner for District 2, a seat he has held since 2021. He served in the Colorado House (District 26) from 2006 to 2013 and the Colorado Senate (District 22) from 2013 to 2019, and he previously taught in Jeffco Public Schools.

79 endorsements

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Ballot position 3

Greg Ketcham

Unaffiliated

Greg Ketcham is an unaffiliated candidate for Jefferson County Assessor. His campaign says he is a certified general appraiser who has worked as a residential and commercial appraiser in the Jefferson County Assessor's Office since 2017, and that he was born and raised in Littleton.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Worth knowing

Open seat. Current Assessor Scot Kersgaard is not on the ballot. Andy Kerr is now the District 2 county commissioner; he won the Democratic primary over Deputy Assessor Sue Flageolle.
