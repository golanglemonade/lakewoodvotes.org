Colorado statewide offices

Secretary of State

Who votes
All Lakewood voters
How to vote
Vote for one · 4-year term

What the job is

Oversees Colorado elections, business filings, and charities and lobbyist registrations, and keeps the statewide voter list.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio; Wikipedia; The Green Papers

Who's running

5 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Amanda Gonzalez

Democratic Party

Amanda Gonzalez, a Democrat, is the Jefferson County clerk and recorder, an office she won in 2022. An attorney, she was executive director of Colorado Common Cause from 2018 to 2021 and is an adjunct law professor at the University of Denver.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio; Wikipedia

Ballot position 2

James Wiley

Republican Party

James Wiley is the Republican nominee and a former executive director of the Libertarian Party of Colorado who switched parties to run. He holds a degree and an MBA from CSU-Pueblo, has worked as a business consultant, and has been involved in election-related lawsuits and advocacy, including founding a group called the Union of Ballot Counters.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio; Wikipedia

Ballot position 3

Sean Vadney

Libertarian Party

Sean Vadney, of Highlands Ranch, is a musician and small business owner who has been involved with the Libertarian Party of Colorado since 2019 and has served as its communications director. The party's vacancy committee named him its nominee after primary winner Alex Astley withdrew.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Wikipedia

Ballot position 4

Amanda Campbell

American Constitution Party

Amanda Campbell is the American Constitution Party nominee for secretary of state, an office she also ran for in 2018 and 2022. A Colorado native, she describes herself as a former business owner.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Ballot position 5

Celeste Landry

Forward Party

Celeste Landry, of Boulder, is the Forward Party nominee. She has worked as a math teacher and a manufacturing production scheduler, and has been a volunteer lobbyist on election issues, including with the League of Women Voters, and a leader of groups that advocate for alternative voting methods.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Worth knowing

This is an open seat. Secretary of State Jena Griswold (Democrat) is term-limited and is running for Attorney General. Amanda Gonzalez is the current Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder. Sean Vadney took the Libertarian spot after the party's first nominee withdrew.
