Congressional District 7

Regent of the University of Colorado

Who votes
All Lakewood voters
How to vote
Vote for one · 6-year term

What the job is

The nine-member Board of Regents governs the University of Colorado's four campuses. It sets tuition and the budget, approves major contracts and tenure, and oversees the university president.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio (1, 2)

Who's running

3 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Paul Mueller

Republican Party

Paul Mueller, a Republican from Leadville, is a senior research fellow at the American Institute for Economic Research and owns a bed and breakfast in Leadville. He holds a Ph.D. in economics from George Mason University and previously taught at The King's College in New York City.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio

Ballot position 2

Nolbert Chavez Incumbent

Democratic Party

Nolbert Chavez, a Democrat from Lakewood, has represented the 7th Congressional District on the University of Colorado Board of Regents since 2021. He is chief of external initiatives and executive director of CityCenter at CU Denver, and served four terms in the Colorado House of Representatives from 1995 to 2002.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio

Ballot position 3

BetteRose Ryan

Libertarian Party

BetteRose Ryan, a Libertarian, is an Army veteran who studied mathematics at Metropolitan State University of Denver. She was a Libertarian candidate for Colorado State Senate District 20 in 2022.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio

Worth knowing

Nolbert Chavez was first elected in 2020. Regents can serve no more than two terms. The at-large regent seat is not on the 2026 ballot. Only Districts 2, 6, and 7 are up this year, and only District 7 covers Lakewood.
