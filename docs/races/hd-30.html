State House District 30

Colorado State Representative

Who votes
Lakewood voters in House District 30 (about 80,500 Lakewood residents; this district is almost all Lakewood, plus Edgewater)
How to vote
Vote for one · 2-year term

What the job is

State representatives write and vote on Colorado's laws and the state budget. The House has 65 members, all elected every two years.

Sources: Colorado Secretary of State; Colorado Independent Redistricting Commissions (1, 2); Colorado General Assembly; Ballotpedia

Who's running

2 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Rebekah Stewart Incumbent

Democratic Party

Democratic state representative for House District 30 since January 2025 (elected 2024), running for re-election. She is a board-certified music therapist and a former member of the Lakewood City Council.

6 endorsements · $63,932 raised

Campaign website

Sources: Colorado Secretary of State (1, 2); Colorado General Assembly; Ballotpedia; Rebekah Stewart campaign

Ballot position 2

William Tyler Switzer

Republican Party

Republican candidate for House District 30. His campaign says he lives in Lakewood, holds a master's degree in public policy and has worked in business, including startups; it lists no prior elected office.

$4,793 raised

Campaign website

Sources: Colorado Secretary of State (1, 2); GoodParty.org

Worth knowing

Rebekah Stewart has held this seat since January 2025.
