Colorado statewide offices

State Treasurer

Who votes
All Lakewood voters
How to vote
Vote for one · 4-year term

What the job is

Manages and invests the state's money, and runs the program that returns unclaimed property, such as forgotten bank accounts, to its owners.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio; Wikipedia; Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck

Who's running

4 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Jeff Bridges

Democratic Party

Jeff Bridges, a Democrat from Greenwood Village, has represented Senate District 26 in the Colorado Senate since 2019 and serves on the legislature's Joint Budget Committee. He served in the Colorado House from 2017 to 2019 and earlier worked for U.S. Sen. Ken Salazar and for Union Theological Seminary.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Wikipedia

Ballot position 2

Kevin Grantham

Republican Party

Kevin Grantham, a Republican from Cañon City, has been a Fremont County commissioner since 2021. He served in the Colorado Senate from 2011 to 2019, including as Senate president in 2017 and 2018, and served on the Joint Budget Committee.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Wikipedia

Ballot position 3

Jodie Barr

Libertarian Party

Jodie Barr is the Libertarian nominee for state treasurer and, according to Colorado Politics, has run a small business and studied state and municipal finance.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Ballot position 4

Marilee Langner Sturgis

Approval Voting Party

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Worth knowing

This is an open seat. Treasurer Dave Young (Democrat) is term-limited. Jeff Bridges is a state senator. Kevin Grantham is a Fremont County commissioner and a former state senator.
