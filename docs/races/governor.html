Colorado statewide offices

Governor and Lieutenant Governor (voted on together)

Who votes
All Lakewood voters
How to vote
Vote for one · 4-year term

What the job is

The governor runs state government, proposes the state budget, signs or vetoes bills, and appoints judges and agency heads. The lieutenant governor takes over if the governor cannot serve. You cast one vote for a governor and running-mate team.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio; Denver Gazette; Colorado Politics; Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck; The Green Papers

Worth knowing

This is an open seat. Gov. Jared Polis (Democrat) is term-limited. Phil Weiser, the current Attorney General, won the Democratic primary over Michael Bennet. Victor Marx narrowly won the Republican primary over Barb Kirkmeyer and Scott Bottoms. Weiser's running mate, Lesley Dahlkemper, is a Jefferson County commissioner. Greg Lopez is a former Republican member of Congress who is running unaffiliated. No candidate on the ticket is the current governor or lieutenant governor.
