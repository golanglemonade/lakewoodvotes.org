2026 ballot › Federal offices › U.S. Representative

Congressional District 7

U.S. Representative

Who votes
All Lakewood voters (Lakewood is in Congressional District 7)
How to vote
Vote for one · 2-year term

What the job is

Represents this district in the U.S. House of Representatives. House members vote on federal laws and the federal budget, and help people who have problems with federal agencies.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio; Wikipedia; Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck

Who's running

6 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Tim Bennett

Republican Party

Tim Bennett is the Republican nominee and a retired U.S. Army officer who served nearly 30 years and retired in 2018. He was elected in 2025 to the Lewis-Palmer School District 38 Board of Education and lives in Monument.

Ballot position 2

Brittany Pettersen Incumbent

Democratic Party

Brittany Pettersen, a Democrat, is the incumbent U.S. representative for the 7th Congressional District, first elected in 2022, and is seeking a third term. She previously served in the Colorado House of Representatives (2013-2019) and the Colorado Senate (2019-2023). She grew up in and lives in Jefferson County.

4 endorsements · 2 answers in their own words · $1,866,765 raised

Worth knowing

The winner serves in the 120th Congress. The county's sample ballot also lists Districts 1, 2, and 6, but those districts cover other parts of Jefferson County, not Lakewood.
