Federal offices

United States Senator

Who votes
All Lakewood voters
How to vote
Vote for one · 6-year term

What the job is

One of Colorado's two members of the U.S. Senate. Senators vote on federal laws, the federal budget, treaties, and the president's nominees for judges and cabinet posts.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio (1, 2); Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck; The Green Papers

Who's running

6 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Mark Baisley

Republican Party

Mark Baisley is a Republican state senator from Senate District 4 who previously served in the Colorado House; he sought the Republican nomination for governor before switching to the U.S. Senate race in January 2026. He is an aerospace engineer who has worked for NASA and defense contractors, and lives in Woodland Park.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio

Ballot position 2

John Hickenlooper Incumbent

Democratic Party

John Hickenlooper is Colorado's incumbent Democratic U.S. senator, elected in 2020, and is seeking a second term. He was governor of Colorado from 2011 to 2019 and was elected mayor of Denver in 2003. Earlier he worked as a geologist and opened a brewpub in Denver's Lower Downtown.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio; The Green Papers

Ballot position 3

Bob Chew

Forward Party

Bob Chew is the Forward Party nominee for U.S. Senate; he changed his registration from Republican to Forward in 2025. He is a former Navy nuclear submarine officer and engineer who co-founded an engineering and technical services company in 1996 and served as its CEO. He lives in Boulder.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Ballot position 4

Christopher Baum

Approval Voting Party

Christopher Baum is the Approval Voting Party nominee for U.S. Senate. A candidate profile describes him as a computer consultant and Navy veteran.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Ballot position 5

Blake Huber

Libertarian Party

Blake Huber is the Libertarian nominee for U.S. Senate, an election-reform advocate and co-founder of the Approval Voting Party who says he has worked more than 30 years in management, and he has previously served as a Libertarian Party state chair and national committee member. He was the Approval Voting Party's vice-presidential candidate in 2016, its Colorado secretary of state candidate in 2018, and its presidential candidate in 2020 and 2024 (with running mate Andrea Denault in 2024).

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Ballot position 6

Adam Withrow

Unity Party

Adam Withrow is the Unity Party nominee for U.S. Senate.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Worth knowing

Yes, Sen. John Hickenlooper's seat is on the 2026 ballot. He won the Democratic primary over Julie Gonzales, and Mark Baisley was the only Republican on the primary ballot. The ballot has a blank line for a write-in.
