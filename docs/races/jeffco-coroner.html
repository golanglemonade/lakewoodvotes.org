Jefferson County offices

Jefferson County Coroner

Who votes
All Lakewood voters
How to vote
Vote for one · 4-year term

What the job is

The coroner looks into sudden, violent, or unexplained deaths and decides the cause and manner of death.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder; BallotReady; The Critical Report

Who's running

2 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Nick Lobello

Democratic Party

Nick Lobello is the Democratic nominee for Jefferson County Coroner. He is a former chief deputy coroner (eight years, per his campaign) and a board-certified medicolegal death investigator; his campaign also lists past work as a firefighter/paramedic and a licensed practical nurse.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Ballot position 2

Allison Harmon

Republican Party

Allison Harmon is the Republican nominee for Jefferson County Coroner and has cited her experience as a nurse. She announced on June 1 that she was withdrawing because she does not hold the certification a 2024 state law (HB 24-1100) requires of coroners in larger counties, but she did not file to remove her name and remains on the ballot; the county Republican chair says the party wants her to stay in to challenge the law in court.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Worth knowing

Open seat. Current Coroner Annette Cannon's term ends December 31, 2026, and she is not on the ballot. Nick Lobello is a former chief deputy coroner; Allison Harmon is a nurse.
