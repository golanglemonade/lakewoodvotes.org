Jefferson County offices

Jefferson County Sheriff

Who votes
All Lakewood voters
How to vote
Vote for one · 4-year term

What the job is

The sheriff runs the county jail, patrols unincorporated parts of the county, and provides court security and wildfire response. Lakewood itself has its own city police.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder; The Ballot Brief; The Critical Report

Who's running

2 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Regina "Reggie" Marinelli Incumbent

Democratic Party

Reggie Marinelli, a Democrat, has been Jefferson County Sheriff since January 2023 and is seeking re-election. According to the Sheriff's Office, she has worked there for more than 36 years, with supervisory roles in patrol, detentions, investigations and support services, and she commanded the West Metro Drug Task Force from 2012 to 2015.

$6,028 raised

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder; The Critical Report; The Ballot Brief

Ballot position 2

Randall Doizaki

Republican Party

Randall Doizaki is the Republican nominee for Jefferson County Sheriff. His campaign says he is a Marine Corps veteran and a retired lieutenant with more than 23 years at the Arapahoe County Sheriff's Office, and that he is currently a lieutenant in Denver Public Schools' Department of Climate and Safety.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder; Jefferson County Republican Party

Worth knowing

Sheriff Marinelli has held the office since January 2023. Weston Imer won the June Republican primary, but Randall Doizaki is the Republican on the certified sample ballot.
