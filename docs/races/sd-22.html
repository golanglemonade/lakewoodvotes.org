State Senate District 22

Colorado State Senator

Who votes
Lakewood voters in State Senate District 22 (about 90,000 Lakewood residents; the district also takes in Wheat Ridge, Golden, Applewood and Edgewater)
How to vote
Vote for one · 4-year term

What the job is

State senators write and vote on Colorado's laws and the state budget. The Senate has 35 members.

Sources: Colorado Secretary of State (1, 2); Colorado Independent Redistricting Commissions (1, 2); Colorado General Assembly; Ballotpedia

Ballot position 2

Monica Irasema Duran

Democratic Party

Democratic state representative since 2019, for House District 24 (2019-2023) and House District 23 (2023-present), and House Majority Leader since 2023; she is term-limited in the House and running for the Senate District 22 seat held by term-limited Sen. Jessie Danielson. She previously served on the Wheat Ridge City Council and worked in the dental industry for more than 30 years.

72 endorsements · $108,131 raised

Campaign website

Sources: Colorado Secretary of State (1, 2); Colorado General Assembly

Worth knowing

Open seat. The current senator, Jessie Danielson (Democrat), is not on the 2026 ballot for this seat. Monica Duran now serves in the state House (District 23). Lakewood is split between Senate Districts 20 and 22.
