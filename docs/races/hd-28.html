State House District 28

Colorado State Representative

Who votes
Lakewood voters in House District 28 (about 28,500 Lakewood residents; the district also takes in Dakota Ridge and Columbine)
How to vote
Vote for one · 2-year term

What the job is

State representatives write and vote on Colorado's laws and the state budget. The House has 65 members, all elected every two years.

Sources: Colorado Secretary of State; Colorado Independent Redistricting Commissions (1, 2); Colorado General Assembly; Ballotpedia

Who's running

1 candidate · ballot order

Ballot position 1

David Rein

Democratic Party

Democratic candidate for House District 28, the seat Rep. Sheila Lieder is leaving to run for the state Senate. He is the Lakewood City Council member for Ward 4, first elected in November 2023, and a lawyer; he previously served six years on the Lakewood Advisory Commission.

8 endorsements · $54,206 raised

Campaign website

Sources: Colorado Secretary of State (1, 2)

Worth knowing

Open seat with one candidate on the ballot. The current representative, Sheila Lieder (Democrat), is running for State Senate District 20 instead. A David Rein serves on Lakewood City Council (Ward 4); the city's page does not mention a state House run, so the guide should not assume they are the same person until confirmed.
