State Senate District 20

Colorado State Senator

Who votes
Lakewood voters in State Senate District 20 (about 67,000 Lakewood residents; the district also takes in Arvada, Dakota Ridge, Fairmount and Evergreen)
How to vote
Vote for one · 4-year term

What the job is

State senators write and vote on Colorado's laws and the state budget. The Senate has 35 members.

Sources: Colorado Secretary of State (1, 2); Colorado Independent Redistricting Commissions (1, 2); Colorado General Assembly (1, 2)

Who's running

2 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Richard (Rich) Mancuso

Republican Party

Republican candidate for Colorado Senate District 20. His campaign says he is a U.S. Army veteran who served as a combat medic, worked in financial services, taught American history at D'Evelyn Junior/Senior High School, and has chaired the Jefferson County Liquor Board.

$11,716 raised

Campaign website

Sources: Colorado Secretary of State (1, 2)

Ballot position 2

Sheila Lieder

Democratic Party

Democratic state representative for House District 28 since January 2023, elected in 2022 and re-elected in 2024; she is running for the Senate District 20 seat held by Sen. Lisa Cutter, who is not seeking re-election. Ballotpedia lists a career in the telecom industry and work as legislative and political director for the Communications Workers of America in Colorado and Wyoming.

9 endorsements · $114,840 raised

Campaign website

Sources: Colorado Secretary of State (1, 2); Colorado General Assembly

Worth knowing

Open seat. The current senator, Lisa Cutter (Democrat), is not on the 2026 ballot for this seat. Sheila Lieder now serves in the state House (District 28). Lakewood is split between Senate Districts 20 and 22.
