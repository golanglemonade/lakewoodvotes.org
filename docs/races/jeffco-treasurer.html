Jefferson County offices

Jefferson County Treasurer

Who votes
All Lakewood voters
How to vote
Vote for one · 4-year term

What the job is

The treasurer collects property taxes, sends the money to the county, cities, schools, and special districts, and invests county funds.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder; Jefferson County; The Ballot Brief

Who's running

2 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Eric Proctor

Democratic Party

Eric Proctor is the Democratic nominee for Jefferson County Treasurer. He describes himself as a community banker and a former public school teacher, and he has held Democratic Party posts including Jefferson County Democratic Party secretary.

1 endorsement

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Ballot position 2

Murl Hendrickson

Libertarian Party

Murl Hendrickson is the Libertarian Party candidate for Jefferson County Treasurer. His campaign says he lives in Arvada, works as an associate financial representative, holds a B.A. in economics from the University of Colorado, and is completing Certified Financial Planner certification.

4 endorsements

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2)

Worth knowing

Open seat. Current Treasurer Jerry DiTullio (in office since 2018) is not on the ballot. No Republican appears on the official sample ballot for this office.
