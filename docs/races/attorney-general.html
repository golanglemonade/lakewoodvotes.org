Colorado statewide offices

Attorney General

Who votes
All Lakewood voters
How to vote
Vote for one · 4-year term

What the job is

The state's top lawyer. Represents Colorado in court, gives legal advice to state agencies, and enforces consumer-protection and other state laws.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Colorado Public Radio; Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck; Wikipedia

Who's running

2 candidates · ballot order

Ballot position 1

Michael J. Allen

Republican Party

Michael J. Allen, a Republican, has been district attorney for the 4th Judicial District (El Paso and Teller counties) since 2021; he was first elected in 2020 and re-elected in 2024. He served eight years in the U.S. Navy and worked as a consumer protection attorney in the Kansas Attorney General's Office.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck

Ballot position 2

Jena Griswold

Democratic Party

Jena Griswold, a Democrat, has been Colorado secretary of state since 2019 and is term-limited in that office. An attorney who grew up in Estes Park, she previously practiced international anti-corruption law in Washington, D.C., and directed the Colorado governor's Washington, D.C., office.

Campaign website

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck; Colorado Public Radio

Worth knowing

This is an open seat. The current Attorney General, Phil Weiser, is running for governor. Jena Griswold is the current Secretary of State. Only two candidates are on the ballot.
