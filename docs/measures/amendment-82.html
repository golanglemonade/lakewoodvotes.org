State of Colorado · Amendment 82

82 Right to buy and sell natural gas

A YES vote means
Creates a state constitutional right to buy and sell natural gas for use in homes and businesses.
A NO vote means
Current laws and rules about buying and selling natural gas stay the same.

In plain words

This would add a right to the state constitution for homes and businesses to buy natural gas for heating and cooking. It would also protect the right of gas distributors and utilities to sell it. It could limit how much the state and local governments can restrict natural gas, for example through building codes.

Sources: Colorado Secretary of State (1, 2); Colorado Legislative Council Staff

Money

Small state costs that were not estimated, mostly for the Department of Local Affairs and legal and regulatory work. Local governments with building codes that limit gas could face higher legal and compliance costs.

Who votes and what it takes to pass

Who votes: All registered voters in Colorado, including all Lakewood voters

Needs: 55% (constitutional amendment)

How it got on the ballot: Citizen initiative (petition signatures); filed as Initiative #177
