West Metro Fire Protection District · Ballot Issue 7C

7C Fire district sales tax for emergency medical and fire services

A YES vote means
A new 0.75% fire district sales tax is added (75 cents on a $100 purchase) to fund fire and emergency medical services.
A NO vote means
No new fire district sales tax.

In plain words

This would add a new 0.75% sales tax inside the West Metro Fire district. Groceries, gas and medicine would be exempt. The money would pay for emergency medical and fire response, staffing, safety gear for firefighters and paramedics, rescue equipment, and wildfire response. The district says it faces a $40 million funding gap in 2027 and has not asked voters for new money in over 20 years. A citizen board would review spending monthly, with a yearly independent audit.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); West Metro Fire Protection District (1, 2)

Money

Up to $36.3 million in fiscal year 2027, then whatever the tax raises after that. The county's tax notice includes written comments against it, but they are not signed by a named group.

Who votes and what it takes to pass

Who votes: Voters living inside West Metro Fire Rescue's district. The district says it serves the City of Lakewood. Check your sample ballot to confirm.

Needs: Majority

How it got on the ballot: Referred by the fire district board
