City of Lakewood · Ballot Question 2H

2H When new mayor and council terms begin

A YES vote means
New mayor and council terms start at the first regular council meeting after results are certified.
A NO vote means
The charter's current wording on when terms start stays the same.

In plain words

This changes the city charter to say that a newly elected mayor or council member starts their term at the first regular council meeting after election results are certified. The city says this lets council hold its first November meeting instead of canceling it. The city says the actual swearing-in date would not change. If passed, it takes effect Jan. 1, 2027.

Sources: City of Lakewood (1, 2); Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder

Who votes and what it takes to pass

Who votes: All City of Lakewood voters

Needs: Majority

How it got on the ballot: Referred by city council (Ordinance O-2026-25, Aug. 10, 2026, 11-0 vote)
