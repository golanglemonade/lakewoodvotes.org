Front Range Passenger Rail District · Ballot Issue 7A

7A Sales tax and borrowing for Front Range passenger rail

A YES vote means
A new 0.333% district sales tax is added and the district may borrow up to $580 million for Front Range passenger rail.
A NO vote means
No new rail district sales tax or borrowing.

In plain words

This would add a 0.333% sales tax (about a third of a cent per dollar) in a district that runs along the Front Range and includes Lakewood. It would also let the district borrow up to $580 million. The money would build and run a planned passenger train, called the Colorado Connector, between Fort Collins and Pueblo. Service between Denver and Fort Collins is planned first. The legislature redrew the district in 2026 to include named cities, including Lakewood.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder; Colorado General Assembly; Ballotpedia News; Complete Colorado

Money

About $295 million a year at first, rising later. Up to $580 million in debt, with total repayment up to about $785 million.

Who votes and what it takes to pass

Who votes: All Lakewood voters (state law SB26-172 names Lakewood as fully inside the district)

Needs: Majority of all votes cast across the whole district

How it got on the ballot: Referred by the Front Range Passenger Rail District board (Aug. 28, 2026, 14-1 vote)
