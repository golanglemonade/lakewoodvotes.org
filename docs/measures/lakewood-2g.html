City of Lakewood · Ballot Issue 2G

2G City sales tax increase for public safety and traffic safety

A YES vote means
The city sales tax goes up by half a cent per dollar (to 3.5%), with the money set aside for public safety and traffic safety.
A NO vote means
The city sales tax stays at 3%.

In plain words

This would raise Lakewood's city sales and use tax from 3% to 3.5%. Food, including groceries, would not pay the added half-cent. The new money could only be used for public safety (including crime prevention and homelessness response) and traffic safety (streets, sidewalks, slowing traffic, and walking and biking safety). The city could keep and spend all of this money without TABOR limits. The city says the tax would be the first increase in about 20 years.

Sources: Jefferson County Clerk and Recorder (1, 2); Jeffco Transcript / Colorado Community Media; City of Lakewood (1, 2)

Money

Up to $17.6 million in the first full year, then whatever the tax raises after that. That is 50 cents on a $100 taxable purchase. Food for home use is exempt from the increase.

Who votes and what it takes to pass

Who votes: All City of Lakewood voters

Needs: Majority

How it got on the ballot: Referred by city council (Aug. 24, 2026, 8-2 vote)
