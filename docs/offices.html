Colorado legislature

5 races

Lakewood is split among several districts. You vote in one House district and one Senate district.

Which districts are yours?

Your address is sent only to the U.S. Census Bureau's address lookup to find your districts. We don't keep it.

Which districts cover Lakewood

  • Under the maps drawn in 2021, Lakewood is split among three state House districts: 23, 28 and 30. House District 30 has the most Lakewood residents. Sources: Colorado Independent Redistricting Commissions (1, 2)
  • Lakewood is split between two state Senate districts: 20 and 22. Both are on the 2026 ballot. Sources: Colorado Independent Redistricting Commissions; Colorado Secretary of State

Jefferson County offices

7 races

Everyone in Lakewood votes on these.

About county races

  • Jefferson County has three commissioners. Each must live in one of three commissioner districts, but every county voter votes for every commissioner seat. Terms are four years. Sources: Jefferson County (1, 2)
  • If Commissioner Andy Kerr wins the assessor race, his District 2 commissioner seat would become vacant. If Lesley Dahlkemper is elected lieutenant governor, she would leave her District 3 seat before her term ends. Source: Denver Gazette
